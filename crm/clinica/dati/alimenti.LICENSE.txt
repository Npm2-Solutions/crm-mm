Table de composition nutritionnelle des aliments Ciqual 2025
================================================================

Source: Anses. 2025. Ciqual French food composition table.
https://ciqual.anses.fr/ - https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17550133
Version of 3 November 2025, sheet in English ("Table Ciqual 2025_ENG_2025_11_03"),
taken on 2 October 2026.

The data are reused under the Licence Ouverte / Open Licence of Etalab
(https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/), as the French Agency
for Food, Environmental and Occupational Health & Safety (ANSES) makes them
available: the source and the date of the version are mentioned with them, their
sense is not altered, and the reuser alone is responsible for the reuse.

What NPM2 Solutions Srl derived from them, as the licence allows:

- the names of the foods translated into Italian by NPM2 Solutions Srl (`name`);
  ANSES's English name is kept beside each one (`name_en`);
- of each food, only energy (kcal, Regulation EU 1169/2011), proteins,
  carbohydrates, fats and fibre for 100 g; where the table gives no energy, it is
  computed from the nutrients with the factors of the Regulation EU 1169/2011
  (`kcal_computed`);
- "traces" and values under the limit of quantification count as 0; a value the
  table does not give stays empty;
- each food in one of the library's groups, from CIQUAL's categories.

The numbers are ANSES's. ANSES does not endorse DottorCloud and is not
responsible for this reuse.
